Quran Reels Publisher

Quran Reels Publisher is a web-based content creation and publishing tool for creating short vertical Quran recitation videos. The service helps an authorized user generate 9:16 short-form videos containing Quran verses, reciter attribution, captions, visual backgrounds, and relevant metadata. Users may connect their own social media accounts and explicitly authorize supported publishing integrations before any content is uploaded or published.

About the Service

A specialized vertical video generator built for Quranic reflections and reminders.

Quran Reels Publisher is an independent software application designed to assist content creators and channel administrators in producing respectful, high-quality vertical videos (9:16 aspect ratio) suited for modern short-form video platforms such as TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Clear & Independent Tool

Quran Reels Publisher is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or associated with TikTok, ByteDance, YouTube, Google, or any reciters or institutions. Content creators are solely responsible for ensuring they possess the necessary rights and permissions for any recitations, imagery, or audio they choose to publish.

How It Works

A structured, user-directed workflow from verse selection to final publishing.

1

Selection & Scheduling

The authorized user selects or schedules a specific Surah, complete ayah range, and preferred reciter style.

2

Vertical Video Generation

The application renders a 1080×1920 (9:16) video with precise word-by-word timing, high-contrast typography, and peaceful background aesthetics.

3

Metadata & SEO Preparation

Standardized metadata is prepared, including Quranic verse references, Arabic and translated captions, reciter attribution, and relevant tags.

4

Quality Review & Staging

Every generated item passes strict quality checks (dimensions, duration bounds, audio balance) before being staged into a secure review queue.

5

Explicit OAuth Publishing

The authorized user reviews and publishes the content or saves it as a draft to connected platform accounts using official developer APIs.

Content Creation Standards

Respectful design focused on clarity, accessibility, and fidelity to Quranic recitation.

Full Verse Integrity

The system enforces whole-verse boundaries, avoiding mid-sentence cuts or fragmented meanings so that recitations preserve their intended context.

High-Clarity Typography

Rendered with verified Uthmani fonts, subtle text backdrops, and dynamic contrast adaptation to ensure effortless readability on mobile displays.

Balanced Audio Mastering

Audio is carefully balanced and mastered to meet broadcast loudness standards (-14 LUFS) with natural acoustic warmth and zero distortion.

Platform Integrations & Publishing

Secure, transparent publishing powered exclusively by official platform APIs.

Quran Reels Publisher integrates with supported social media platforms exclusively through official, documented developer APIs (including the TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API v3).

User-Granted OAuth Only

No account access occurs without explicit user consent. We never request, collect, or store your social media account passwords.

Direct Post or Draft Staging

Depending on the authorized platform scopes (e.g. video.publish or video.upload), creators can publish directly or upload as drafts for review inside the platform app.

Revocable at Any Time

Creators maintain complete ownership of their accounts and can revoke integration permissions at any moment directly from their platform account security settings.
