Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026 • Effective Date: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Quran Reels Publisher ("we", "us", or "our") operates an independent content creation and publishing tool designed to help creators create and publish short-form vertical Quran recitation videos to connected social media platforms.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our service. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and safeguard data when you use the Quran Reels Publisher application and its publishing integrations.

2. Information We Process

We only process the minimal information necessary to deliver the functionality requested by the user. Depending on the features you configure and authorize, this may include:

  • Content Configuration & Metadata: Selected Surah and Ayah ranges, reciter names, Arabic script text, English translations, video captions, hashtags, and visual background preferences.
  • Generated Media Files: Temporary and finalized vertical video files (1080×1920) created for publishing or review.
  • Authentication Credentials & Tokens: Scoped OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens granted explicitly by you to connect your destination accounts (e.g., TikTok or YouTube).
  • Publishing Records: Technical logs recording the date, time, platform status, and published post/video identifier returned by the destination API.

3. Connected Accounts & Authorization

Quran Reels Publisher interacts with third-party social media platforms exclusively when explicitly authorized by the account owner.

No Password Collection: We never ask for, collect, or store your passwords for TikTok, YouTube, Google, or any other external platform. All authentication takes place directly on the third-party platform’s official OAuth consent screens.

4. TikTok Authorization & Integration Details

If you choose to connect your TikTok account using our TikTok Developer App integration:

  • Explicit Consent: Access is established strictly via TikTok's official OAuth authorization flow where you explicitly grant permission.
  • Scoped Access: The application requests only the specific scopes required for content management, such as video.publish (to publish videos directly to your feed upon request) and/or video.upload (to upload videos as drafts to your TikTok inbox for manual review prior to posting).
  • Purpose Limitation: Access tokens are used only to perform authorized API actions on your behalf (such as uploading the video file, title, and creator settings specified by you).
  • No Passive Access: The application does not monitor your private messages, browsing history, or unrelated account activity.

5. How Information Is Used

The information we process is used strictly for:

  • Generating short-form vertical Quran videos according to your configuration.
  • Transmitting authorized video files and metadata to your connected social media channels.
  • Providing status updates, error handling, and confirming completion of scheduled publishing jobs.
  • Preventing duplicate publications within configured time windows.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.

Data is transmitted only to the third-party platforms that you explicitly instruct the application to connect with (such as TikTok via the TikTok Content Posting API or YouTube via the YouTube Data API v3). When content is submitted to those platforms, it becomes subject to the respective platform’s privacy policy and terms of service.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

OAuth tokens and publishing records are retained only as needed to provide the connected publishing functionality or until access is revoked by the user.

Temporary render files and local workspace artifacts are periodically purged once video rendering and publishing confirmation have completed. If you disconnect an integration, the associated OAuth credentials and tokens are removed.

8. Data Security

We employ standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect OAuth tokens, credentials, and media files against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All network communications with third-party platform APIs are conducted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

9. User Choices & Revoking Access

You remain in full control of your third-party integrations at all times:

  • TikTok: You can revoke our application's access at any time by navigating to your TikTok account settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services and removing Quran Reels Publisher.
  • YouTube / Google: You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account Third-party apps with account access page.

Upon revocation, our application will immediately lose the ability to perform any actions on your account.

10. Third-Party Services & Links

Our application interacts with third-party APIs. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

11. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for content creators and channel administrators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our features, platform API requirements, or applicable regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
